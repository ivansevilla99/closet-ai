Closet AI

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Closet AI is built to work on your iPhone. Most of what it does never leaves the device, and what does leave is set out here plainly.

There is no account

We don't ask for an email, a password or your real name. There is no sign-up and no login. The name you type during onboarding is only used to greet you and never leaves your iPhone.

What never leaves your device

  • Your photos and the cut-outs of your items.
  • Your wardrobe: items, outfits, tags, wear calendar.
  • Where you keep your clothes. That says something about your home, and it stays on the phone.
  • Your style profile and what the app learns from your decisions.
  • Internal usage statistics, which are local counters.

What does leave, and when

Studio finish (Closet AI Pro)

If you turn on the studio finish, scanning an item sends the photo to our service, which passes it to OpenAI's model to separate the garment from the background and describe its attributes. The photo is not stored on our service: it is processed and discarded. OpenAI's handling is governed by their terms for API use.

With the studio finish off, no photo leaves your iPhone.

Usage limits

To apply scan limits and control cost, we store in our database, for each scan made with the studio finish:

  • your device's vendor identifier (the one iOS provides, different for each developer and erased when you uninstall the app),
  • the date and duration of the scan,
  • the model used and the cost in tokens.

We don't store the photo, the garment, or anything describing your clothes.

Weather

If you enable location, the app sends Open-Meteo a coordinate rounded to two decimal places — a little over a kilometre — at most once an hour, to find today's temperature. We don't store that coordinate or any location history. Without permission, the app estimates the weather from the time of year.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking networks.
  • There is no third-party analytics: no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, nothing similar.
  • We don't build advertising profiles or track you across apps.

Your rights

Since there is no account, control is direct: Settings → Delete the whole wardrobe removes everything the app stores, and uninstalling takes the rest. If you want us to delete the usage records tied to your device, write to us and we will.

If you live in the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation applies. Data controller: Iván Sevilla. Contact: ivan.sevilla.ruano@gmail.com.

Children

Closet AI is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Significant changes will be announced inside the app.
