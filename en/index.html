Closet AI

Your wardrobe, organised and considered.

Photograph an item and the app cuts it out, recognises it and files it. Every day it suggests a look from what you already own.

What it does

  • Scan an item with the camera and cut it out on a transparent background.
  • Recognise its type, colour, pattern, style and season.
  • Suggest a look each day for the weather and the occasion.
  • Learn from what you pick and what you skip.
  • Note where you keep each item so you can find it later.

Where it happens

Cut-out and classification run on your iPhone, with Vision. Offline and at no cost. Only the Closet AI Pro studio finish needs to send the photo to our service.
